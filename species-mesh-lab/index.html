Botanical design notes

Dense basal tuft, arching blue-green folded blades and tall slender flowering stems. Panicles change from relatively contracted to more open as they age; dry tones are illustrative. Regional ID Lab records cite Hagenbuch & Lemke (2015), p. 54, and Diggs et al. (1999), pp. 1286, 1288. The tiny ligule, hairs and variable awns are not modeled; the leaf thickness, number of blades and spikelet simplification are artistic and performance choices. The stage selector is not a calendar forecast.
