Lindheimer’s muhly — design notes

Broad fountain habit, dense basal tuft, folded blue-green tapering blades and tall slender culms with narrow panicles that loosen at maturity. Varied blade curvature, thickness, twist and muted dry foliage. Sources: Wildflower Center · Grass ID Lab record (Hagenbuch & Lemke 2015 p.54; Diggs et al. 1999 pp.1286,1288). Ligules and microscopic hairs omitted.

Little bluestem — design notes

Narrower bunch, more upright leafy stems, short separate racemes terminating leafy branches, paired spikelet shapes and silvery bristles. Fall and winter show copper to straw tones. Sources: Wildflower Center · Grass ID Lab record (Hagenbuch & Lemke 2015 p.60; Diggs et al. 1999 p.1316; Gould 1975 pp.608–609). Racemes use approximate 2.5–5 cm lengths; hair and awn geometry is stylized. No particular variety or cultivar is claimed.

Individual seeds control plant structure. Lower detail removes components and widens fine geometry for legibility. Early, mature and dry stems share their basic arrangement, but minor geometry changes with stage and detail. This isolated preview does not change the main designer or saved projects.
